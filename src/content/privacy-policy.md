**PRIVACY POLICY**



Your privacy matters to us.



Last Updated: 27 September 2026



The Sumit Bijarnia Foundation (“Foundation”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.



*We believe privacy should be clear, responsible and easy to understand. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use our website, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when we may share it, how long we may keep it, and the choices and rights available to* you.



This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website and related digital interactions with the Foundation.



⸻



1\. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT



We collect personal information only when it is reasonably necessary for a specific purpose connected with our website, activities or services.



You may provide information to us when you:



\* contact us through our website;

\* submit an enquiry, feedback or request;

\* ask about volunteering;

\* express interest in a partnership or collaboration;

\* make or enquire about a donation;

\* subscribe to Foundation communications, where available;

\* participate in Foundation programmes, activities or events;

\* communicate with us by email, phone or other official channels;

\* interact with us through our official digital or social-media channels; or

\* otherwise voluntarily provide information to us.



Information you may provide



Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:



\* Full name

\* Email address

\* Mobile or phone number

\* Postal or residential address

\* City, state and PIN code

\* Organisation or professional details, where relevant

\* Information included in your enquiry or communication

\* Volunteer, partnership or collaboration information

\* Donation amount

\* Donation date and transaction/reference details

\* PAN or other applicable identification information, where required for donation records, statutory reporting or tax documentation

\* Photographs, videos, stories or testimonials, where voluntarily provided or collected with appropriate permission in connection with Foundation programmes or activities

\* Any other information reasonably necessary to respond to your request or provide a relevant service



Please provide only information that is relevant to your interaction with us.



Information collected automatically



When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website’s configuration and the services we use.



This may include:



\* IP address;

\* browser type and version;

\* device type and information;

\* operating system;

\* pages visited;

\* date and time of visits;

\* referring website or source;

\* general website usage information; and

\* cookie and similar technology information.



This information may help us maintain website security, understand general website usage, improve website performance and improve your experience.



You can generally browse without directly providing personal information



You can generally visit and browse our website without directly submitting personal information.



However, certain features—such as contacting us, making a donation, requesting information or participating in certain activities—may require specific information.



If you choose not to provide information that is necessary for a particular request or service, we may not be able to process that request or provide the relevant service.

2\. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION



We use personal information only for legitimate, relevant and necessary purposes connected with our activities, website and services.



Depending on how you interact with us, we may use your information to:



\* respond to enquiries, questions and requests;

\* communicate with you about Foundation activities;

\* respond to volunteering, partnership and collaboration requests;

\* process donations;

\* maintain accurate donation and organisational records;

\* issue donation receipts;

\* provide applicable tax documentation;

\* complete applicable statutory, regulatory and tax reporting;

\* provide information or resources you have requested;

\* communicate with you about Foundation programmes, activities or updates where appropriate;

\* manage participation in Foundation programmes or activities;

\* coordinate with volunteers, partners, professionals or organisations where necessary;

\* improve our website, content and user experience;

\* understand general website usage and performance;

\* maintain website security and prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorised activity;

\* comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and administrative requirements;

\* protect our rights, users, partners and the Foundation; and

\* maintain appropriate organisational records.



We will not use your personal information for purposes that are unrelated to the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted or required by applicable law or otherwise appropriately authorised.



⸻



3\. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS HANDLED



We aim to handle personal information carefully and use only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.



Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the legality of processing that took place before the withdrawal.



If you withdraw consent, we may no longer be able to provide a particular service or respond to a request where the information is necessary for that purpose.



Where we send optional communications, you may also choose to unsubscribe using the available unsubscribe option or by contacting us.



We may continue to retain or process certain information where this is necessary or permitted under applicable law—for example, for statutory records, accounting, tax reporting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.



We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.



⸻



4\. DONATIONS AND PAYMENT INFORMATION



When you make a donation through our website, we may collect information such as:



\* your name;

\* email address;

\* mobile number;

\* address;

\* donation amount;

\* donation date;

\* transaction or payment reference;

\* PAN or other applicable identification information where required; and

\* other information reasonably necessary for donation records, statutory reporting, receipts or applicable tax documentation.



Payment processing



Donations may be processed through an authorised third-party payment or donation service provider.



When you make a payment, payment-related information such as card, banking or other payment details may be processed directly by the applicable payment provider in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices.



The Foundation does not intend to collect or store your complete card number, CVV, PIN, OTP, banking password or similar confidential payment credentials unless specifically required, lawfully permitted and securely handled for a legitimate purpose.



We will never ask you to share:



\* OTPs;

\* PINs;

\* CVVs;

\* banking passwords;

\* card passwords; or

\* other confidential banking credentials.



If a payment provider processes your information, its own privacy policy and terms may also apply.



Donation records and tax documentation



We may use the information you provide to:



\* confirm and record your donation;

\* issue a donation receipt;

\* maintain accounting and statutory records;

\* complete applicable donation reporting;

\* provide applicable tax documentation; and

\* respond to donation-related questions or transaction issues.



Please ensure that information provided for donation records and tax documentation is accurate.

5\. CONTACT FORMS AND COMMUNICATIONS



When you contact us through a website form, email or another official communication channel, we may collect the information you choose to provide.



This may include:



\* name;

\* email address;

\* phone number;

\* organisation or professional information, where relevant;

\* reason for contacting us; and

\* the contents of your message or enquiry.



We use this information to understand and respond to your communication and, where relevant, to coordinate your enquiry with the appropriate person or team.

6\. COOKIES AND ANALYTICS



Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.



Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on your browser or device that can help a website remember settings and understand how the website is being used.



Depending on the website configuration, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:



\* enable essential website functionality;

\* maintain website security;

\* improve website performance;

\* understand general website usage;

\* measure website traffic and performance; and

\* improve user experience.



Where third-party analytics or similar technologies are used, the relevant third-party provider may process certain technical or usage information according to its own privacy policy.



Depending on your browser or device settings, you may be able to control, restrict or delete certain cookies.



Disabling some cookies may affect the functionality or performance of parts of the website.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:



\* unauthorised access;

\* misuse;

\* alteration;

\* disclosure;

\* loss; and

\* other reasonably foreseeable security risks.



We limit access to personal information to people and service providers who reasonably need it for legitimate purposes.

9\. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION



We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.



We may retain information for longer where necessary to:



\* maintain appropriate organisational or donation records;

\* comply with legal, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements;

\* complete statutory reporting;

\* resolve disputes;

\* investigate or prevent fraud or misuse;

\* meet other applicable legal requirements.



When personal information is no longer reasonably required and there is no legal or legitimate reason to retain it, we aim to securely delete, anonymise or otherwise appropriately dispose of it.



Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

10\. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS



Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.



These may include the right to:



\* Access — request information about the personal information we hold about you;

\* Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;

\* Deletion — request deletion of personal information where legally permitted;

\* Withdraw consent — withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;

\* Information — ask how your personal information is being used; and

\* Raise a concern or complaint — contact us regarding the way your personal information is handled.



Some requests may be subject to legal limitations or exceptions.



For example, we may need to retain certain information to comply with legal, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements even if you request its deletion.



Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw consent. We will explain any relevant consequences where withdrawal means that we can no longer provide a particular service or respond to a request.



How to exercise your rights



To make a privacy-related request or complaint, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below.



Please include enough information for us to understand and respond to your request.



We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests, particularly where the request involves access, correction or deletion of personal information.



We aim to respond to privacy requests within the time required by applicable law.



If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy-related request or concern, please contact us again so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.



Where applicable, you may also have the right to approach the relevant authority or forum available under applicable law.
